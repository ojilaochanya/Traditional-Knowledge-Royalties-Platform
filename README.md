# 🌿 Traditional Knowledge Royalties Platform

Welcome to the fairest way to protect and compensate indigenous communities for their traditional knowledge on the Stacks blockchain! This project ensures communities get paid for commercial use of their cultural heritage.

## ✨ Features

🔐 Register traditional knowledge with a unique hash  
💰 Auto-distribute royalties to community wallets  
⏰ Immutable proof of ownership and licensing  
📝 Store and verify TK details instantly  
🚫 Prevent unauthorized use with penalties  
🗳️ Resolve disputes through community voting  
📊 Track earnings and usage transparently  

## 🛠 How It Works

**For Communities**  
- Generate a SHA-256 hash of your traditional knowledge (e.g., a recipe or design)  
- Call `register-tk` with:  
  - Your knowledge hash  
  - A title  
  - A description  
  - Community details  
Boom! Your knowledge is now protected on the blockchain, and you can set licensing terms.

**For Commercial Users**  
- Browse registered TK and licensing terms  
- Call `request-license` to pay and use TK  
- Comply with terms or face penalties  

**For Verifiers**  
- Use `get-tk-details` to view registration info  
- Call `verify-tk` to confirm ownership  

That’s it! Fair compensation and transparent tracking.
